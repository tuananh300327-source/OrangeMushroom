##Chess Barrage (game)

Chess Barrage is my very first project, it is a 2d pixel game that is based on Chess where the Enemy pieces wont wait for their turn, instead they will all move based on a timer, the player control the White King solely to move around and shoot a bow to kill enemies. All the enemy troops will try to checkmate the King and if the King remain in Checkmate range for too long, the player will lose, which means the Player have to constantly move their King and find any opportunity to fire the bow and reduce the pressure.

I created this game completely independently as my first project, including all the board and enemy logic, the arts/troops design and animation, except for the music and sound effects which i get from PixaBay

###Main Features
- 6 Levels with differing troops and the last level being a Queen boss fights
- All troops follow the movement and attack range of the their standard chess counterpart, except for the Pawns which can move 4 tiles diagonally around it
- When the King is in checkmate, the tile will flash white and the tile the King and the enemy troop that is threatening the King flash yellow
- A bow attack with animation that require charging and careful positioning/timing
- An enemy reinforcement mechanic where additional enemy troops can spawn in after the ones on the board dies.
